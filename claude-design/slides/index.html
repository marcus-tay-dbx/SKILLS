Data design pattern
The medallion architecture refines data through Bronze, Silver, and Gold
Bronze
raw · as-ingested
Raw data landed exactly as received from source systems — append-only, full history, no transformation.
Silver
cleaned · conformed
Filtered, de-duplicated, and joined into a validated, queryable enterprise view of core business entities.
Gold
curated · business-ready
Aggregated, project-specific tables powering BI dashboards, reporting, and ML features.
Why it works
Each layer is reproducible from the one before it — so you can reprocess, audit, and trust every number back to its raw source.
